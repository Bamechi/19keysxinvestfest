The SuperMind Process

Standing upsell: $100+ → Meet & Greet bundle
1Smile. Real energy, first three seconds.
2Ask what they're looking for.
3Recommend ONE product.
4Explain one or two benefits. Not ten.
5Answer questions straight.
6Invite the purchase. Then the bundle.

Booth Law

Non-Negotiables On The Floor.

Square Only

Every team member carries a Square reader. No general cash. Cash-only attendee? Route through a Floor Manager or team lead — recorded in the same system.

All Sales Final

No refunds once product changes hands — including allergy or preference concerns raised after purchase. Say it up front: items are non-returnable; if it's not for them, gift it.

Stocked & Clean

Organized, clean, fully stocked booth all event hours. That's everyone's job, not leadership's.

Family Standard

Modest, high-level energy for the women representing the brands. All-hands, gentlemanly standard for the men — move equipment, cover stations, help wherever needed.

We run this as a family environment, not a weekend gig. Battle-tested and battle-ready for the long lines — because Keys' booth pulls the longest lines at InvestFest, and that pressure peaks during M&G windows.
